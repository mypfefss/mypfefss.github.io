Privacy Policy

MyPFE collects the information you enter in the registration form (name, email, phone, university, level and, optionally, your CV) to organize the event and, with your consent, to share your CV with partner companies for recruitment.

CVs are stored in a private Google Drive account managed by the MyPFE organizers. We do not sell your data. Access is limited to organizers and, with your consent, partner companies.

To access or delete your data, contact us at mypfe@fss.usf.tn.
